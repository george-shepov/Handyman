Services

Useful work, clearly scoped.

The site is deliberately specific. That helps customers—and search systems—understand which projects are a good fit.

01

Decks and outdoor upkeep

Replace a few damaged boards, tighten or repair railings, pressure wash decks and prepare surfaces for the next maintenance step.

Best for repair and maintenance, not structural rebuilds.

02

Assembly and mounting

Furniture assembly, shelving, curtain rods, mirrors, pictures, TV mounts and common household hardware.

Wall type and item weight are checked before installation.

03

Doors, trim and adjustments

Sticking doors, loose handles, minor trim repair, caulking, weather-stripping and small finish-carpentry corrections.

Photos usually help establish the scope quickly.

04

Household punch lists

Combine several small tasks into one organized visit: patches, hardware replacement, touch-ups, installation and minor repairs.

Grouping tasks often gives better value than separate visits.

About

A technically minded, hands-on approach.

Giorgiy “Gio” Shepov is a Richmond Heights homeowner, software developer and practical problem solver with a substantial set of DeWalt tools—and no fear of using them. The goal is not to pretend every job is simple. It is to define the work accurately, document it clearly and complete appropriate projects with care.

For safety and compliance, work requiring permits or a licensed electrical, plumbing, HVAC or structural contractor is not represented as ordinary handyman work.

FAQ

Questions before booking.

What areas do you serve?

Richmond Heights, Lyndhurst, Highland Heights, Mayfield Heights, Beachwood, South Euclid and nearby east-side communities.

Can you quote from photos?

Often, yes. Clear photos, measurements and a description may be enough for a preliminary range. Hidden damage or uncertain conditions may require an on-site visit.

Who buys the materials?

Either arrangement can work. The estimate should state whether the owner supplies materials or whether they will be purchased and billed with receipts.

Do you take emergency calls?

This is primarily scheduled handyman work, not a 24-hour emergency or licensed-trade service.

Can I combine several small jobs?

Yes. A written punch list and photos are the most efficient way to plan a multi-task visit.
